Sleep Lock settings

Firm mode removes the easy way out. From 2 hours before your bedtime until your wake-up time (the locking period): you can’t delay sleep, and turning Sleep Lock or Firm mode off, moving bedtime later, or moving wake-up later all need an Emergency Bypass. Earlier bedtime or wake-up is always free. Outside the locking period, you can still plan ahead freely.

Today only

Change tonight’s bedtime or wake-up without touching your default schedule. It reverts by itself after tonight. Earlier is always free; later follows the Firm mode rules above.

Set up MacroDroid first. Sleep Lock can’t block your phone until a phone token is connected. Create a token in Settings, then import the Cloud Bridge.

Your daily sleep schedule uses Riyadh time (UTC+3), even when you travel.

During these hours, your existing MacroDroid setup blocks your phone. Taskitator can stay closed. Emergency Bypass unlocks it temporarily; ordinary breaks do not override Sleep Lock.

Outside these hours, your normal task and Universe blocking rules apply. Your phone needs internet and MacroDroid’s regular status checks.

Delay sleep for today

Postpone your saved bedtime by 30 or 60 minutes, once per night across all devices. Wake-up time stays the same. An overnight schedule uses the date its bedtime starts, so midnight does not give you another delay. Other task locks still apply.

Firm mode: delays are only available until 2 hours before bedtime, so plan ahead. From then on, delaying needs an Emergency Bypass.
