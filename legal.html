3. Google sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in, Google and Clerk handle the authentication process. Basic identity information made available by that connection, such as your email address, name, and profile picture, may be used to create or link your Clerk account and identify your Taskitator account.

Taskitator uses this connection for sign-in and account linking. The current app does not use Google sign-in to read Gmail messages, Google Drive files, or Google Calendar content. Additional access would require a separately introduced feature and an updated explanation.

Clerk manages passwords and provider sessions. Taskitator does not store your Google password or Clerk password in its application settings. Changing or disconnecting a sign-in method does not by itself delete your Taskitator workspace.

6. Optional AI and API keys

When you use an AI feature with your own key, Taskitator sends the relevant request through its Cloudflare Worker to the active provider: Google’s Gemini service or Anthropic’s Claude service. The Worker processes the request and response in transit. Depending on the feature, a request can contain task information, prompts, proof criteria, conversation context, and images or documents you provide for proof or reference review.

Hosted Taskitator AI (Taskitator Pro)

On Taskitator Pro, AI runs in hosted mode unless you switch to your own key in Settings → Pro. In hosted mode, Copilot conversations, note clean-up, criteria checks and proof reviews, including the photos and PDF files you submit as proof and any reference files, are sent to Anthropic’s Claude service through Mazendev’s own Anthropic account. This happens whichever provider you activated for your own key, including when you activated Gemini and never activated Claude. Your saved keys are not used in hosted mode. To keep AI content with the provider you activated, switch Pro to your own key. Hosted usage is counted against the allowances of your license.

Universe AI reviews use Google’s Gemini service: with your saved Gemini key or, where hosted Universe review is enabled, with Mazendev’s own Gemini key. A review sends the task title and criteria, your proof text and proof files, and the task’s reference files.

Saved Gemini and Claude API keys are stored separately and encrypted using AES-256-GCM in server-side storage. The encryption keyring is held as a separate Cloudflare Worker secret. Requests made with your own key use the active provider’s saved key on the server rather than returning it to your browser. Browsing a provider in Settings does not activate it: use the provider activation button to choose which service receives AI content sent with your own key. Copilot context is reset when providers change, and failures do not automatically route content to a different provider. Hosted Taskitator AI on Pro is the exception described above: it always uses Anthropic. Showing, copying, replacing or deleting a saved key requires a short-lived, one-use password or linked-Google confirmation. Google may accept an existing Google session; Taskitator checks that the verified identity matches the linked account. A revealed key is temporarily held in browser memory, and a copied key may remain in your operating system clipboard.

Before legacy plaintext key fields are removed from attributed browser/cloud copies, the update preserves them encrypted. Browser-only and historical recovered keys require a verified selection before they become active. Unreadable or unattributed records, old exported files and devices that have not run the update may retain plaintext copies and need separate review. Updated cloud snapshots and ordinary exports omit API keys.

This is encryption at rest, not end-to-end encryption. The Worker decrypts a key when needed; an operator or attacker controlling the Worker and its secrets could access it. Connection tests request model metadata without generated tokens, and do not establish generation quota or billing availability.

Google’s and Anthropic’s handling of AI requests depends on the applicable provider terms and the account/API configuration you use. Do not assume AI requests are confidential or excluded from provider retention or improvement practices. Avoid submitting sensitive material you do not want processed by that provider.

7. Cookies, browser storage, and security

Clerk uses its own authentication/session mechanisms, including cookies. Taskitator uses browser local storage and session storage for settings, drafts, synchronization state, and recovery copies; IndexedDB stores local reference files. A service worker caches static application files.

This public information page does not load authentication scripts, analytics scripts, or external fonts, and does not create its own tracking cookies. Hosting providers still process ordinary requests to deliver it.

Authenticated cloud requests require a valid Clerk session and account-ownership checks. Phone tokens are limited to unlock-status access; the server stores their digest. Local drafts and reference files are not encrypted. Logging out normally leaves a copy of your workspace in the browser’s storage; choose “Also erase everything Taskitator stored in this browser” when logging out on a shared computer. Cloud data is not protected by end-to-end encryption. No system can guarantee complete security. Use a separate browser/device profile on shared devices and protect your account and keys.

8. Retention

Current synchronized workspace data and account records are retained while needed to provide the service and until they are removed through account deletion or an available support process. This version does not impose a fixed automatic account-retention period for active accounts.

Universe content. Proof text and proof files are kept until 30 days after the review decision, and longer while a dispute on that decision is open. Proof that has not been decided is kept while its task stays open. Reference files are kept while they are attached to a task. Tasks, comments and the activity history are kept while the Universe exists. When a Universe is deleted, its title, tasks, labels, proof and comment text are erased and its files are removed. Leaving a Universe removes your membership; what you already contributed there stays until it expires as described here or the Universe is deleted.

License and AI usage. The license record and AI-usage counters are kept while your account exists. Records of individual AI reviews are removed about one day after they finish.

Once cloud deletion is accepted, account access is blocked and a retryable cleanup job removes the current workspace, account link, license and AI-usage records, your Universe content as described in section 9, and identifiable legacy records before removing the Clerk login. Necessary cleanup identifiers remain while that job is pending; failures may require operator assistance.

To reduce phone lockout, active phone-token digests are retained as status-only recovery records for seven days from deletion acceptance. They return unlocked status and grant no task access. Minimal hashed deletion receipts and anti-resurrection markers are retained temporarily: receipt/status records expire seven days after completion, and legacy-migration prevention records expire seven days after acceptance. Short-lived verification/replay records are also removed after expiry. Old username/email migration approvals in Cloudflare deployment settings are separate from application data; Mazendev must remove an approval entry manually if it is still present. The deletion status page flags this condition.

Local drafts, recovery archives, cached files, and reference files can remain on other devices after deletion. Legacy records that are unreadable and cannot be linked to an owner are not guessed at or deleted automatically. Providers may retain operational/security records or backup copies under their own policies. A downloaded backup remains wherever you save it until you delete it.

9. Access, correction, and deletion requests

You can edit workspace content in Taskitator, update supported account information through Settings → Manage account & password, and export a backup through Settings.

Delete your cloud account in Taskitator

Open Settings → Data & Backup → Delete account & cloud data. Disable Taskitator blocking macros on every device, verify your password or connected Google identity, check the blocker confirmation, and manually type Macrodroid disabled. Pressing the final deletion button accepts an irreversible cleanup request.

Password confirmation is handled by Clerk. Google confirmation uses an additional authorization flow and checks the Google identity against the one linked to your account; Google may reuse an existing session rather than require a new password. The confirmation is bound to your Taskitator session and a short-lived, one-use action.

After acceptance, cloud access is blocked immediately. The cleanup job removes synchronized workspace data, encrypted API-key and recovered-key records, account/credential links, and identifiable retained legacy copies, then deletes the Clerk login. Identified local archives and reference files in the initiating browser are cleared where possible. The status page reports completion or retrying cleanup.

Universes. Universes you own are deleted for all members and their content is erased. In Universes owned by other people, your membership, your username, the text of your comments, your proof text and proof files, and the reasons you wrote as a reviewer are removed. What remains: your internal account identifier (a random identifier with no name, email or content attached) stays as a blocked marker so the account cannot be recreated inside Universe, and stays on activity-history entries, review decisions and emptied comments in other people’s Universes, where you appear as “Member”. Backup files that an owner or administrator exported before your deletion are outside Taskitator’s control and may still contain your comments.

License. The Taskitator copy of your license record is removed. The licensing service (Keyo) is not updated by this flow: it keeps the link between your license key and your former account identifier. Email info@mazendev.com to have that record removed as well.

Unreadable legacy records with uncertain ownership may require manual review.

Active phone tokens return unlocked status for seven days, then become invalid. Keep blocking disabled. Other devices’ local records, unidentified local recovery copies, downloaded backups, and records retained independently by providers are not erased by this flow.

If you cannot use the deletion flow, email info@mazendev.com from your account email if possible. Include your account email and any old Taskitator username. Do not send passwords, API keys, or phone tokens. Mazendev may verify ownership and explain any limitations before coordinating further removal.

Clearing browser data, the local factory reset, disconnecting Google, or deleting a user directly through Clerk administration is not a substitute for coordinated cloud cleanup. For local deletion, remove site data on every browser/device and delete unwanted downloaded backups; doing so can permanently remove unsynced drafts and reference files.
