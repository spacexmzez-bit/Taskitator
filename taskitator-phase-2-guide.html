2. Configuring Personal Claude API Key

Cloudflare Worker Secret
2

Add CLAUDE_API_KEY to Cloudflare Worker Secrets

  • Log in to the Cloudflare Dashboard at https://dash.cloudflare.com.
  • Navigate to Workers & Pages > click your Worker (taskitator-sync).
  • Click Settings > Variables and Secrets.
  • Under Secrets, click Add:
    • Secret Name: CLAUDE_API_KEY
    • Value: Paste your personal Anthropic API key (sk-ant-api03-...).
  • Click Deploy to save the secret to your Worker runtime.

3. Multi-File Proof Review (1–5 Files)

Core Phase 2 Feature
3

Submitting Multiple Evidence Files for a Single Task

  • On the main tasks screen (index.html), tap the completion circle of an AI-audited task to open the proof submission dialog.
  • The file picker now accepts multiple attachments: select up to 5 files (photos or PDFs).
  • The system validates that individual files do not exceed 5 MiB and the combined batch does not exceed 20 MiB.
  • Single-file review consumes 1 credit; multi-file review (2–5 files) consumes 2 credits.
  • Claude Haiku 5.5 cross-references all attached files together against the criteria before issuing an approval verdict.

4. Batch Proof Review (Up to 20 Tasks)

Core Phase 2 Feature
4

Reviewing Multiple Finished Tasks Against Shared Proof

  • In the page title section of index.html, click the new ⚡ Batch Review button.
  • In the modal that opens, select up to 20 active AI tasks you want to prove simultaneously.
  • Attach up to 5 shared proof files (e.g. photos of a multi-page math assignment or lecture notes covering multiple topics).
  • Click Run Batch Audit. The server evaluates each task independently and charges 1 credit per task reviewed (capped at 10 credits).
  • Review the individual results: eligible tasks are marked ✓ ELIGIBLE and rejected tasks are marked with concise critiques.
  • Click Confirm Completion for Eligible Tasks to commit the completions. The server verifies its 24-hour attestation and marks all confirmed tasks complete in local storage and cloud sync.

5. 45/1000 Credit Quotas & Personal BYOK Toggle

Settings Management
5

Managing Quotas and Switching to Personal BYOK

  • Open Settings in Taskitator. Under the ⭐ Taskitator Pro card, you will see your updated quotas:
    • Daily Credits: 45 (resets at 00:00 Asia/Riyadh).
    • Monthly Credits: 1,000.
  • BYOK Toggle: Check the box Personal BYOK Mode to route task proofs through your own saved provider key in Settings → AI Provider & Saved Keys.
  • Non-Resetting Guarantee: Toggling BYOK preserves your existing hosted credit limits and balances—your hosted credits do not reset or disappear when using your personal key.

6. Google Sign-In & API Key Preview Fix

Security & Identity
6

How the Google Key Preview Fix Operates

  • In Settings → AI provider & saved keys, clicking Show opens the identity confirmation dialog.
  • What was fixed: Previously, clicking Sign in with Google threw a silent 503 error if standalone Google OAuth redirect variables were unconfigured in Cloudflare.
  • How it works now: The updated Worker checks your active Clerk session directly. If your Clerk account has a verified Google login linked, it confirms your identity immediately without requiring external Google OAuth redirect secrets, and enables the Continue button.
  • If Google is not linked to your Clerk profile, a clear red alert is displayed advising you to enter your account password in the field above to reveal your key for 30 seconds.
