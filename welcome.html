Choose Gemini or Claude with your own API key for supported assistance and proof-review features. Relevant content passes through Taskitator’s Worker to the provider you activate. Each provider keeps its own encrypted saved key.

HOW IT WORKS

Your workspace,
across your devices.

Clerk manages sign-in. Cloudflare stores synchronized Taskitator data. Your browser also keeps local drafts and settings.

Opening the app and cloud synchronization require a network connection. Local reference files may stay on the device where they were added.

  1. Sign in securely

    Create an account with email/password or use Google when enabled.

  2. Organize your work

    Add tasks and projects, set up routines, and choose the tools you want to use.

  3. Sync and keep moving

    Synchronize your work between signed-in devices. Review conflicts before choosing which copy to keep.

KNOW YOUR TOOLS

Useful features. Clear responsibilities.

AI can make mistakes. Phone-blocking macros can restrict access if configured incorrectly. Review AI output, keep backups, and test your recovery options before enabling blocking.
