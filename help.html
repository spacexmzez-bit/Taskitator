📅 Today and General Tasks

Today is your committed list. General Tasks is everything.

  1. Open Today from the menu (☰). It lists tasks that are due today or overdue.
  2. A task with no due date never appears on Today. Give it a date to commit to it.
  3. When a dated subtask sits under undated parents, it shows on Today with a small path above it (for example School › Biology). The parents stay hidden. The task keeps its own subtasks.
  4. Open General Tasks from the menu to see every task in its normal hierarchy.
  5. The day rolls over at the time set in Settings → Day & week. Anything you do before that time counts toward the previous day.

A dated task under a dated parent shows inside the parent, not twice.

➕ Create and edit a task

Tap + New task (the + button).

  1. Fill in Task Title. Description, Project, Priority, Tags and Due Date are optional.
  2. Tags: type one and press + Add. You can add several.
  3. The Due Date starts as today only when you create from the Today view.
  4. Tap Save Task.
  5. To change a task later, tap it to open Task Details, edit, then Save Changes.
  6. To move a task under another one, use Hierarchy / Parent Task in Task Details. Its subtasks move with it.

Tag and priority choices are managed in Settings → Tasks.

Title: Write essay outline · Project: School · Due: today · Tag: writing → Save Task. It now appears on Today.

📝 Drafts

Park an unfinished task and come back to it. A draft is not a task: it does not appear on Today, count as overdue or affect your phone lock.

  1. New task: fill in whatever you have and press Draft. Only the title is required. Nothing is created or locked and no AI is used.
  2. Open the menu (☰) and tap Drafts. Press Open to refill the form, or Delete to remove the draft.
  3. Press Save Task on an opened draft to publish it. The draft is removed. Pressing Draft again updates the same draft.
  4. Existing task: in Task Details press Draft to keep your edits without changing the task. When you open that task again a notice offers Restore or Discard draft. Save Changes removes the draft.
  5. Restoring only fills fields you are allowed to change. A locked or shielded task keeps its restrictions.
  6. Drafts sync with your account and are included in backups. You can keep up to 50 new-task drafts.

A draft stores the title, description, due date, project, priority, tags, bonus, shield, AI proof choice, criteria text, Mr. Study fields, Dynamic Priority choice and the subtasks you typed. It does not store the reference file, the criteria validation score (validate again before publishing) or the repeat schedule. Edit drafts do not include subtask changes.

An AI proof choice is only locked when you publish, and its confirmation appears then.

🛡️ Subtasks, Shield and Bonus

Break a task into steps and decide whether the steps are required.

  1. In Create Task or Task Details, type a step in Quick Subtasks and press + (or Enter).
  2. Turn on Require all subtasks first (Irreversible Shield) to stop the parent being completed until every required subtask beneath it is finished. It cannot be undone.
  3. Mark a subtask ⭐ Bonus Subtask to exclude it from the Shield. A bonus never blocks its parent.
  4. To flip several tasks at once, use Multiselect → ⭐ Bonus and choose Make required or Make bonus. Tasks that cannot change (shield-protected required work) are skipped.

The Shield is irreversible: you cannot switch it off afterwards.

☑ Multiselect and batch actions

Change many tasks at once.

  1. Tap ☑ Multiselect, then tick tasks. All selects everything shown.
  2. The bar offers 🏷️ Tags, 🎯 Priority, 📅 Due, ⭐ Bonus, 🗑 Delete and ⚡ Batch review selected tasks.
  3. Tags replaces the current tags on every selected task.
  4. Due date: overdue AI-locked tasks are guarded against postponement.
  5. Delete shows Apply · delete N; nothing is deleted until you press it.

🔍 Filter and sort

Narrow or reorder what you see.

  1. Tap Filter. Choose Projects, Priorities and Tags, then Apply Filters. Parent tasks of matches stay visible for context. Clear All resets.
  2. Tap ⇅ to sort by Priority, Alphabetical, or Required, then priority.
  3. Numbers inside titles are compared as numbers, so Chapter 2 comes before Chapter 10 when sorting alphabetically.

⚠️ Overdue tasks

The banner at the top of Today counts overdue tasks.

  1. The banner offers 📅 Reschedule to date…. Pick a Target Due Date and press Reschedule Tasks.
  2. This moves eligible, non-locked overdue tasks in bulk.
  3. If you have active AI-checked tasks that are past due, rescheduling non-AI tasks is blocked. Complete or roll over the overdue AI tasks first.

📁 Projects

Group tasks. Every account has an Inbox.

  1. Open Projects from the menu. Tap the + chip to create one: name, icon and theme color, then Save Project.
  2. Tap a project chip to switch to it. Tap ✎ to edit the active project (the Inbox cannot be edited).
  3. Deleting a project asks what should happen to its tasks: divert to Inbox, reassign to another project, or delete all tasks with it.

🔁 Repeating tasks

Make a task and its subtasks come back on a schedule.

  1. When creating, set Repeat. Or open a task's details and press Set up recurrence (only an active task can start repeating).
  2. Options: Daily, Selected weekdays, Monthly (start-date day), Date of month, Every six months, Yearly.
  3. Set a Start date and an optional End date.
  4. A subtask follows its parent's schedule. A repeating branch cannot contain another repeating series.
  5. On a repeating task, Apply ordinary task edits to lets you pick This occurrence or This and future occurrences. Due-date changes apply to this occurrence only. Use Recurrence & future criteria to change future dates or requirements.
  6. If you stop repetition you can still finish the current task.

☕ Daily breaks

Tap the break pill (⏸️ Breaks) to open the panel.

  1. You get 3 hours of breaks per day, shared by two systems. The maximum can be lowered in Settings → Day & week.
  2. Each day you use one system. Once you pick one, the other is locked until the day resets.
  3. 📅 Scheduled: press start planning. You get 10 minutes to enter up to 3 non-overlapping breaks within the allowance. Saving locks Scheduled for today; closing without saving cancels.
  4. ☕ Flexible: pick 30 min, 45 min, 1 hour, a custom length, or until your allowance ends. It starts immediately. You cannot end it in its first 10 minutes (or your strict minimum).
  5. Every started minute counts: 30 minutes 1 second uses 31 minutes. A break never continues into the next day.
  6. Optional Strict modes are in Settings → Day & week.

🚨 Emergency bypass

For real emergencies only.

  1. Tap 🚨 Emergency bypass and confirm.
  2. It lifts restrictions for 15 minutes and unlocks editing and deleting of protected tasks.
  3. You get 4 per week. They refill after your weekend. Set weekend days in Settings → Day & week.
  4. It is separate from breaks: it never ends a break or changes your break allowance.

💡 Scratchpad (Distraction Dump)

Park a distracting thought and return to work.

  1. Tap 💡 Scratchpad. A timer starts (default 90 seconds; set 30s–5m in Settings → General).
  2. Write the thought, then press Dump & Return. It also saves when the timer ends.
  3. Notes expire after 48 hours.
  4. 📦 View notes is locked until you complete, move or trash required tasks due today or overdue.
  5. In the notes view you can export as .txt or .md, or delete all.

📸 AI proof verification (your own key)

Require proof before a task can be completed. Needs an AI key (see next section) or Pro.

  1. In Create Task or Task Details, turn on Require AI Proof Verification (Irrevocable). It cannot be turned off afterwards.
  2. Write Proof Criteria: what the proof must show. Press 🔍 Validate Criteria. With the strict setting on (Settings → Tasks) criteria must score 7/10 or higher before the task can lock.
  3. 💡 Recommended opens templates and your saved criteria. Saved criteria need 9/10 or higher; the limit is 6.
  4. Optional: 📎 Attach Reference Exemplar (1 file, up to 5 MB, image or PDF) when the proof must match an example.
  5. To complete the task, submit proof: 1–5 files, 8 MB per photo or 30 MB per PDF, plus an optional note. Proof and reference exemplars together can total up to 40 MB per review. The AI scores it; 7 out of 10 passes.
  6. Submitting consumes AI usage even if the proof is rejected. There are no refunds.
  7. 🧪 Test your criteria (before the task is locked): upload a sample proof and see how the auditor reads your criteria. It runs the same audit as a real proof but saves, completes and locks nothing. It needs 7/10 validation first when strict is on, and costs AI usage like a real audit. Once a task is locked, tests are unavailable unless Emergency Bypass is active.

A passing test does not guarantee the real audit passes. AI verdicts can vary.

Show exampleHide example
Criteria: Photo of 2 handwritten pages showing the full essay outline → Validate → score 8/10 → 🧪 Test your criteria → upload a photo → read the verdict → adjust the wording before saving the task.

🤖 Set up AI with your own key

Copilot, criteria checks, proof reviews and note extraction need a key on Free.

  1. Open Settings → AI & Pro.
  2. Choose a Provider: Gemini or Claude. Each keeps its own key, so switching never erases one.
  3. Press add key and paste a key from Google AI Studio (Gemini) or the Claude Console. You confirm with your password or Google first. Keys are encrypted on Taskitator's server.
  4. Press Test key. It checks the key works and uses none of your quota.
  5. Press the button that makes the selected provider the active one.
  6. Free accounts have a daily and monthly allowance for AI through your key. The card in Settings shows it as Available, Caution or Critical.

📲 Phone lock (MacroDroid)

Android only. MacroDroid blocks distracting apps until today's tasks are done.

  1. Open Settings → Phone lock → Open guide and follow it. Do it once.
  2. Press Create new token. The token starts with tkp_.
  3. Press Download Bridge (with your token) and Download App Blocker. In MacroDroid import the App Blocker first, then the Bridge, and save each.
  4. Do not rename the App Blocker: the Bridge finds it by that title. Keep MacroDroid and your browser out of the blocked list.
  5. The Bridge checks every 5 minutes, when the screen turns on and when MacroDroid opens. Apps unlock when the server says the device is unlocked.
  6. You can only change phone settings when today's tasks are done, during a break, or while Emergency Bypass is on.
  7. Tasks completed offline do not unlock your phone until this device syncs.
Show exampleHide example
Not blocking? Check the App Blocker is enabled, was imported before the Bridge, and still has its original title.

🔄 Sync, backup and account

Where your data lives.

  1. The dot near the top shows sync: gray = this device only, yellow = waiting to sync, green = up to date, red = failed or offline. Force Sync Now sends changes immediately.
  2. If two devices changed the same field differently, a panel asks you to pick This device or Cloud for each. Nothing is uploaded until you press Apply.
  3. Settings → Account & data → Download a backup saves a .json file. To restore choose Merge (adds, deletes nothing) or Replace everything.
  4. Clear browser, clear completed & trash and Delete account are in the Danger zone. Download a backup first.

◆ Hosted AI (no key needed)

On Pro, Copilot, criteria checks, proof reviews and notes use Taskitator AI automatically.

  1. Nothing to set up. Open Settings → AI & Pro to see your plan and remaining credits.

  3. Your allowance comes from your Pro license, with a daily limit and a 30-day cycle limit. Daily limits reset at midnight Asia/Riyadh.

The exact numbers on your account are shown in Settings.

🧾 How credits are charged

Every charge is rounded up to the next 0.5 credit.

  1. Before a review runs you see a price preview. A temporary maximum is held; unused held credits return.
  2. You pay the real cost of the review. There is no multiplier for reference exemplars and no batch discount.
  3. Reviewing several tasks together is one combined request, so it usually costs less than reviewing them one by one.
  4. If the verdict is insufficient proof (legitimate but needs another page or clearer photo) you are charged half the real usage, rounded up to the next 0.5.
  5. Tap View details to see usage to 4 decimals.

⚡ Batch proof review

Review several AI-locked tasks together.

  1. Tap ☑ Multiselect, tick the AI-locked tasks, then ⚡ Batch review selected tasks.
  2. You can include up to 20 tasks per batch.
  3. Attach 1–5 files (8 MB per photo or 30 MB per PDF).
  4. You pay the real cost of the combined review. Check the price before you start.

Reviewing several tasks together usually costs less than reviewing them one by one.

Fast numbered subtasks.

  2. Type a subtask containing {num==1} and press Enter. The number advances each time.
  3. Without Pro, add subtasks one by one. Existing numbered subtasks stay intact.

📉 Dynamic Priority

Lower a task's priority while only bonus work is left.

  1. In Create Task or Task Details open the Dynamic Priority section.
  2. Under While only bonus work remains, use priority choose a level.
  3. The task takes that priority while only bonus subtasks are unfinished, and returns to normal automatically if required work becomes unfinished again.

🌌 Universe

A shared workspace for a group. Open it from the menu.

  1. Tabs: My dashboard, Projects, My stats, Controls (managers) and Settings. Needs an internet connection; changes are not queued offline.
  2. Your tasks are grouped by Overdue, Today, Upcoming, No date and Complete. A task moves through Open → Submitted → In review → Approved or Needs changes (fix and resubmit).
  3. Managers create work with ＋ in Projects and manage People, Invitations (single-use links that reserve a seat) and Labels in Controls.
  4. Limits: up to 20 members besides the owner, 2 Universes you own. You can join 2 Universes on Free or 7 on Pro.
